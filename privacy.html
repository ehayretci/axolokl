Privacy notice

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Axolokl is a student project in the Design Engineering master’s programme at Imperial College London. We’re designing a wheel lock against bike-wheel theft in London. If you have any question about your data, email us at info@axolokl.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist.
  • If you join our demo team: that you joined and when you gave your consent. To send you an Axolokl to test, we’ll ask for your delivery address by email, and then for your input on the product.
  • Your answer to our one question, if you give one: what you mostly use your bike for.
  • If you fill in our bike survey (axolokl.co.uk/survey): your answers, saved as you go, so unfinished surveys are kept too; when you answered; your device type (phone or computer); and the link you came from. It’s anonymous unless you leave your email for an interview. We delete survey answers by April 2027.
  • If you scanned one of our QR tags on a bike: which tag it was. Each tag is unique, so it tells us which bike it was on, and where and when we put it there.
  • Basic visit data: anonymous page-view statistics, which buttons and links you click on this site (for example opening the waitlist or joining the demo team; if you join the waitlist, these are linked to your email), and the technical logs our website host keeps, such as your IP address. We don’t use cookies for this.

Answering the question is optional, and you can skip it.

Why we collect it

  • To send you your 25% discount code when Axolokl launches. That’s the only email we plan to send, unless you join our demo team.
  • If you join our demo team: to contact you by email so you can test Axolokl for free before launch and help shape it with your input.
  • For market research for our university project: to understand who wants Axolokl, what they use their bikes for, and which bikes and places they use.

Our legal basis

  • Emails: your consent, which you give when you join the waitlist. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the email or by emailing us.
  • Demo team: your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you join. We record when you ticked it. You can withdraw at any time by emailing us.
  • Your answers (including the survey) and tag data: your consent. Answering is optional, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who else sees it

  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website and stores the waitlist and your answer.
  • Umami, which gives us anonymous visit statistics without cookies.
  • Google (Gmail), which we use to send the launch email.
  • Module staff at Imperial College London, who may see our results. We share results as totals, not as individual answers.

We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

International transfers

Our providers are based in the United States and may store data there. Cloudflare protects transfers from the UK with the Information Commissioner’s international data transfer addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, as set out in its data processing agreement. Umami and Google may also process data outside the UK, under their own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only for the length of the project and delete all of it by 1 July 2027.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Just email info@axolokl.co.uk. We make no automated decisions about anyone.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Cookies and analytics

We don’t use cookies. Our analytics tool, Umami, counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. If you arrive from a QR tag, we keep the tag’s code in your browser for that visit only, so your sign-up stays linked to the tag. It’s cleared when you close the tab.
